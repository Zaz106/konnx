Low-Voltage Electrical Connectors

Rated for safety and reliability, KonNx Low-Voltage electrical connectors are available in lightweight aluminium and stainless steel materials.

The range features coupling systems with operating voltages ranging from 660V to 3,300V and rated up to 450A. Plugs and sockets in the low-voltage range are suitable for use across opencast and underground mining and tunnelling sites.
