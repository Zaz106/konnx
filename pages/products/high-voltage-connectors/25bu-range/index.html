25BU Range

25,000V | 800A

Operating voltage of 25,000V and 800A rating. Based on the design of the 22kV coupler, the 25BU is a safe, reliable option for Non-Ex sites that operate high-voltage machinery. It offers full phase to phase segregation with earth, arc-extinguishing phase insulation, and an enclosed guide pin for protection against external damage.
